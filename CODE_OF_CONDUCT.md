# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically honest project.

Contributors should be constructive, assume good faith, respect privacy, credit others' work, and keep examples safe and synthetic. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publication of private assessment data or credentials, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel; do not expose sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly, consistently, and without retaliation.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, issue trackers, and other spaces where someone represents CheatyKitty.
